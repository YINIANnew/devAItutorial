### English

#### Tutorial Introduction
This is a complete tutorial on AI-powered medium-to-large scale engineering development for individuals and small teams. It explains the full workflow of AI-assisted full-stack development in plain, approachable language.  

> Why build this tutorial series?  
To put it bluntly: "vibe coding" has been popularized by clickbait content creators, and many people have followed suit to build toy projects — but they cannot deliver real, production-grade applications. This tutorial is strictly practical knowledge that teaches you how to do real full-stack development. AI is meant to improve efficiency, not to make up for lack of expertise.  
Simply put: AI has no inherent full-stack development capability. Anyone who truly understands AI knows that it is fundamentally a computational module built on linear regression. It has no real comprehension, nor can it reliably resist hallucinations. Combined with the fundamentally flawed attention mechanism of Transformer architectures, there is no AI that can independently develop a large-scale project. At least for now, it is simply not feasible.
This tutorial does not chase hype or stir up trends. It is written in straightforward language, and even readers with no prior background can follow along if they are willing to learn.  

> What does "medium-to-large scale project" mean?  
This tutorial teaches you to build medium and large-scale applications. Streaming platforms, for example, generally fall into the large-scale category, while medium-scale projects are more robust than basic demos. You might think even the most powerful AI cannot pull this off. But here is the truth: AI *can* help you achieve it — it all depends on the person using it. The problem is not AI itself; it is the lack of proper planning and orchestration on the human side. In other words: if you just sit back and let AI do everything, you will end up with nothing to show for it.  

#### Target Audience
This tutorial is for **anyone who wants to genuinely learn AI-assisted full-stack development**, regardless of prior experience. That said, existing full-stack skills are highly recommended, as they give you far more control and oversight in practice.  

It is **not intended for casual learners, people using it for entertainment, or anyone just looking to show off**. If that is what you are after, you are free to look elsewhere.  

The writing style is relaxed rather than dryly academic — technical subject matter is serious enough on its own, so there is no need for overly formal prose. Anyone willing to learn can understand the content. Technical terms are explained in detail with examples, making it accessible even to total beginners. That said, prior knowledge helps; if you start from zero, we recommend learning basic full-stack fundamentals after finishing this tutorial.

#### 教程介绍
这是全套个人/小团队使用AI进行中大型工程项目开发的教程，以通俗易懂的语言来讲述使用AI进行全栈开发的全流程
> 为什么要搞这么一套东西出来？  
虽然话不好听，还是直说吧，因为现在营销号带起来了VibeCoding，然后很多网友跟风创作出了自己的玩具，但是却做不出真正的项目，此教程是完全的干货，告诉你怎么做真正的全栈开发，AI是提升效率的而不是弥补无知的，直白来说就是这样，AI没有任何全栈能力，如果有了解真正AI的会知道，AI纯粹就是线性回归的一个计算模块，既没有理解力也没有幻觉抵抗力，再加上transformer一塌糊涂的注意力机制，哪个AI能真的独立开发出来一个大工程？至少在目前为止，不现实。这个教程完全不跟风不带节奏，而且语言通俗，只要想看，无基础也可
> 中大型工程是什么意思？  
这个教程是教你怎么做中型和大型工程的，像那些流媒体平台，基本都属于大型工程，至于中型就是稍微好一点吧，我相信，你的AI再强，也不能做到吧？但是我想告诉你，AI能做到，但是要看人，不是AI不好，而是人没有做真实的统筹规划，换句话来说，做甩手掌柜早晚变成光杆司令
